# Group 4: Scenario Arena

Scenario Arena consolidates six tests around a repeatable decision frame:

1. What changed?
2. Which authored parts are affected?
3. What is the consequence?
4. Which alternative is likely to reduce exposure?

Earthquake, fire, wind and rain launch the existing visual test after showing the evidence. Only one continuous scenario effect is activated by the arena at a time. Supplier failure and late design change remain lightweight evidence views and open the existing metrics rather than introducing decorative simulation.

The alternative comparison is an indicative exposure reduction, not a structural, fire, weather, procurement, or schedule prediction.
